A wide-brimmed pointed witch hat in deep navy (#1B2340), the brim as wide as her shoulders, the tall cone tip bending back toward her left, with a plain dark band and one small silver buckle; a thin semi-transparent navy veil hangs from the back of the brim down to her waist. A wide dark leather belt. An asymmetric two-layer skirt: a short dark-navy inner skirt at mid-thigh, and an outer layer of sheer navy chiffon that is short in front and falls long on her left side to below the knee, its hem embroidered with the same faint silver script and floating slightly. Bare legs, short black leather ankle boots ending just above the ankle with low block heels. Signature weapon worn on her LEFT hip, angled behind her: an elegant antique rapier with a long, very slim straight blade and a tarnished silver swept hilt with curved bars, a knuckle bow and a shallow cup guard, sheathed in a long narrow dark slate-blue leather scabbard with two silver buckle straps near the mouth; the lower third of the scabbard flares into a bundle of twisted black twigs bound with dark cord, so the sheathed rapier reads as a witch's broom.
