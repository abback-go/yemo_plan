=== CHARACTER ANCHOR ===
이 문단은 모든 프롬프트에 한 글자도 바꾸지 않고 들어간다 (캐릭터 일관성의 핵심).

ESKA, the Witch of Endings: a tall, slender woman in her late twenties, about 7.5 heads tall with long legs (not exaggerated), carrying a cold, detached, otherworldly and melancholic presence. A soft, slightly youthful oval face that still clearly reads as an adult woman, large drooping half-lidded eyes with heavy tired upper lids and faint bluish shadows beneath them, thin pale lips, cool porcelain skin, always expressionless, with a fixed, unblinking, unsettling stare. Her irises are pale ice-blue and softly glowing yet hazy and hollow, with no highlights or catchlights in her eyes, giving an empty, obsessive look. Very long straight hair in dark desaturated steel blue (#3E5068) with lighter steel-blue highlights (#7F93AD), falling to mid-thigh, its ends gently lifting and drifting as if weightless, with no wind; long uneven bangs cover most of her right eye, side locks reach her chest; a few loose stray strands fall across her face and cheeks, slightly disheveled. A wide-brimmed pointed witch hat in deep navy (#1B2340), the brim as wide as her shoulders, the tall cone tip bending back toward her left, with a plain dark band and one small silver buckle; a thin semi-transparent navy veil hangs from the back of the brim down to her waist. An off-the-shoulder fitted navy long-sleeved blouse with a straight neckline that bares her collarbones and shoulder line; the sleeves are slim on the upper arm and flare wide at the wrists, so long that they cover her hands down to the middle of her fingers, no puffed shoulders; the neckline is edged with faint silver-thread embroidery of tiny handwritten script. A thin semi-sheer ash-blue knit shawl draped low across her back and upper arms, resting at elbow height so both shoulders stay bare, its frayed fringe hanging to her hips and drifting softly. A high-waisted fitted dark navy leather corset belt with thin silver lacing, cinching her waist just below the bust. A two-layer skirt: a short dark-navy inner skirt at mid-thigh, and a long sheer outer overskirt of layered navy chiffon with a faint cool silver metallic sheen, split open at the front so her bare legs and the short inner skirt stay visible, flowing down at the sides and back to her ankles, its hem embroidered with the same faint silver script and floating slightly. Bare legs, short black leather ankle boots ending just above the ankle with low block heels. Signature weapon on her LEFT hip, attached to her belt by leather straps and angled backward so the scabbard end trails behind her: a slim, straight double-edged silver longsword about two-thirds of her height, with a narrow blade tapering to a sharp point and a central fuller etched with faint lines of tiny handwritten script, silver at rest and glowing ice-blue when she attacks; an ornate silver cross-guard whose arms curve and twist like thorny branches, set with a small pale ice-blue gem at its center; a grip wrapped in dark leather with a small pointed silver pommel; a long dark slate-blue leather scabbard with an ornate silver throat plate and two silver buckle straps near the mouth; the lower third of the scabbard flares into a bundle of twisted black twigs bound with dark cord, so the sheathed sword reads as a witch's broom. The fingertips of her left hand, peeking out from the sleeve, are stained black, and thick black ink slowly drips from them. A few faint lavender motes of light drift around her. Strictly cool palette: navy, steel blue, pale skin, ice-blue glow (#9FD8FF), soft lavender accents (#B9A2FF). No warm colors.
